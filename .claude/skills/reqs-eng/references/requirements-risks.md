# Requirements risks

This document lists and describes the main risks to watch out for when practicing requirements
engineering. ALWAYS watch out for and prompt the user once something like this is detected.

## Insufficient user involvement

Insufficient user involvement increases the chances that the things that are actually developed end
up not being what the user needs.

What is insufficient also depends on the context. Some projects may not require constant customer
communication. Regardless, these are some symptoms to watch out for:

- The customers think their involvement is unnecessary and is "wasting their time"
- The developers are dealing with surrogates and not actual user representatives
- Uncertainty for _current scope_ features is high, the user is giving a lot of answers that are
  similar to "I don't know" or "I need to talk to someone else about this"

## Ambiguous requirements

Ambiguous requirements leads to different expectations and frustrations from stakeholders. Here are
symptoms to watch out for:

- A reader can interpret a requirement statement in several ways.
- It is difficult to derive validation and acceptance tests from requirements

## Gold plating

This is unnecessary addition of functionalities that weren't initially in the requirements due to it
"looking cool" or because "customers are going to love it". Creative solutions are always welcome,
but just winging it leads to waste of resources. The main thing to watch for here is features that
aren't traceable to a business requirements justification.

## Overlooked stakeholders

Overlooking stakeholders means that some user's needs won't be met.
